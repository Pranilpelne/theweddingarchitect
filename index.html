The Wedding Architect is a reflection of everything we stand for, a passion project turned purpose, built on the belief that love deserves to be documented with truth, elegance, and emotion.

Since 2016, we have been crafting timeless wedding films and photographs that do more than just capture moments; they hold the essence of a day that can never be repeated.

Based in Nagpur, Maharashtra and trusted across borders, our journey has taken us to destination weddings, intimate gatherings, and grand celebrations, each one deepening our understanding of how love moves and manifests.

WHAT DO WE DO?

Photography

From capturing your wedding day to creating enchanting couple portraits, or even taking memorable family photos at your ancestral home, we're here to snap those precious moments. Let's not forget those heart-racing surprise proposals or the thrill of eloping – we've got you covered there too! Anything that tugs at your heartstrings, we transform into stunning visuals that you can treasure forever.

Film

What's cooler than photos? Animated photos! We all go gaga over pictures, but imagine adding a dash of beautifully crafted film and hand-picked music to create a masterpiece that takes you back to your special moments like they just happened yesterday, It's like having a magic time machine in your pocket!

Afterall , this is the only proof left of all the craziness that went into planning the special day!
