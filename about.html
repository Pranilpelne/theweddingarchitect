ABOUT THE WEDDING ARCHITECT

Origin & Philosophy

An architect by education. An artist by perspective. A storyteller by instinct.

"Where architecture meets emotion, and every story finds its own form."
Pranil Pelne Founder & Creative Director

The story of The Wedding Architect began with a simple DSLR camera and a curiosity to see beyond what meets the eye.

My journey began in architecture, where I learned to understand space, composition, light, proportion, colour, and the relationship between people and their surroundings. What started as a formal education gradually became a way of seeing the world — one that would eventually lead me into photography, filmmaking, and the art of storytelling.

Over the past eight years, that perspective has evolved into something far greater than I initially imagined.

Today, The Wedding Architect is a creative house built around the belief that every story deserves its own visual language. From grand, high-profile celebrations to intimate weddings filled with personal meaning, we approach every project with the same curiosity, sensitivity, and artistic intention.

The Collective

Our work extends beyond weddings into fashion editorials and architectural photography, allowing us to explore different visual worlds while bringing a distinctive perspective to each. Whether we are documenting a fleeting emotion, shaping the visual narrative of a fashion project, or capturing the character of an architectural space, our approach remains rooted in composition, authenticity, and detail.

What began as one person with a camera has grown into a team of 10 creative minds, united by a shared commitment to thoughtful storytelling. As a photographer, curator, and wedding director, I continue to shape not just the images we create, but the entire visual experience behind every project.

Honors & Awards

Awarded by @WedMeGood • WeddingSutra Award 2024 • Over 500+ Weddings Documented

Our journey has also taken us across India and international destinations, with prestigious recognition including being awarded by @WedMeGood, winning the WeddingSutra Award 2024, and documenting over 500+ celebrations with enduring artistic depth.

But beyond the projects, publications, and milestones, the purpose remains the same: to create work that feels considered, personal, and enduring.
